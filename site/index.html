FAQ

Questions

What happens to my order history?

Shelfie keeps the items, not the order. Once your shelves are filled, the imported text is gone, and your items stay on your device.

How accurate are the stock estimates?

Shelfie works from when you bought each item and how long it usually lasts. You can correct any count in a tap, and it learns from that.

Is there an app for iPhone and Android?

Yes. The iPhone and Android apps are due on 10 January 2027.

Something else on your mind?

Write to hello@homeshelfie.com. Questions, feedback and removal requests all welcome.
