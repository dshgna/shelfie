Privacy

Your cupboard stays yours.

  • What Shelfie keeps

    Your items live on your device. If you sync, they're encrypted first, so our server can't read them.

  • What it never does

    No shop passwords. No selling your data. No ads based on what you buy.

  • Leave any time

    Export everything, or delete everything.

  • When AI helps

    AI import and the shopping assistant only run when you ask. They send only what's needed, and nothing is ever used for ads.

Questions

How accurate are the stock estimates?

Shelfie works from when you bought each item and how long it usually lasts. You can correct any count in a tap, and it learns from that.

Is there an app for iPhone and Android?

Yes. The iPhone and Android apps are due on 10 January 2027.
